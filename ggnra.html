Golden Gate National Recreation Area

2026. MapLibre and custom PMTiles.

I've recreated much of the look and feel of the flagship National Park Service basemap, Park Tiles Standard, using a lightweight MapLibre implementation with custom PMTiles running under the hood. In this early prototype, I'm limiting the geographic scope to Golden Gate National Recreation Area.
